P2 · single-leg wall rig

Position of the FKP triangle relative to the glass

Both figures share one scale. Poses are computed by leg_ik from the config.py calibrated on 2026-07-19; the FP edge can be checked directly with a tape measure.

How to read the figures C = coxa axis (hip) · F = femur axis · K = knee axis · P = center of the cup lip (free state). FK = 80 and KP = 120 are constants; FP changes with the knee angle — measure FP with a tape and θ follows, which is the principle of the three-side calibration. The solid blue line K→P is the model's virtual line; it differs from the real cup axis (blue dash-dot) by a constant δ = 22.7°: in Fig. 1 the cup axis is 4.5° off the normal, and by Fig. 2 it has rotated exactly to perpendicular. From Fig. 1 to Fig. 2: the knee opens 18.6°, the foot advances 35 mm along the glass normal, and C and F never move (this is the "step straight into the glass" mechanism — the pose is guaranteed by the triangle itself, not by alignment). In Fig. 2, P is the kinematic target point; the 15 mm past the glass is the compression of the bellows cup (13.5 preload travel + 1.5 preload).
