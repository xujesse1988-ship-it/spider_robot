TL;DR: one month covering P2 single-leg adhesion acceptance → P3 assembly and 18-channel calibration → P4 pneumatics and electrics → the first wall test. On the wall it got stuck on "stepping in place" for four days (08-17 ~ 08-20), until on 08-19 the user himself named the root cause — the elastic energy stored in the lifted leg is released at the instant of detachment and pulls the body down — and on 08-20 the experiment nailed it quantitatively (83% of the slip happens at the instant the vented seal ruptures), the same day settling on the "zero-force handover before vent" scheme and finishing its implementation (--handover). All three key breakthroughs came from pushing a wrong assumption back with a photo of the real part or with measured data: the tibia three-view correction (08-07), the USB3.0 undervoltage pattern (08-16), the elastic-energy root cause (08-19).
