Pin-level wiring between the Pi 5 and each component

The system diagram is topology level; this one goes down to "which wire goes into which pin". The real pump/valve GPIOs come from software/hexapod/adhesion.py (Pi5VacuumIO, fitted in P1): valve L1 = GPIO5, pump A = GPIO20; servo relay = GPIO17 (driver.py, settled 08-15, previously Servo2040 A0/GPIO26); the other 6 MOSFET control lines are a suggested allocation (settled at P4 assembly), avoiding the I2C1 / UART / SPI0 / ID-EEPROM / PWM-audio pins.

USB: Pi USB-A → Servo2040 (/dev/ttyACM0, chica firmware); servo power does not pass through the Pi. Relay coil DC+/DC− go to the 5V/5A buck output (same source as the Pi, ~100 mA), no header pins used; control takes only Pin 11.
Rules
  • Single-point ground: the 12 V / servo power ground and the Pi signal ground meet exactly once, at the MOSFET board, nowhere else — a loop = ground current corrupting ADS readings.
  • Power-up order: power only the Pi first → vcgencmd get_throttled must be 0x0 → then close the servo relay and the 12 V rail; re-check after any rewiring.
  • No breadboards / Dupont in P4: header wiring with shrouded crimped ribbon or soldered + heat-shrink; MOSFET control lines and 12 V power lines run in separate bundles.
  • Valve control level: adhesion.py measured VALVE_ON_LEVEL=0 (low = cup connected to vacuum) — in P4 measure all six valve channels before writing the map.
