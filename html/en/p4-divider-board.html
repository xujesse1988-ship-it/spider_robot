Spider · P4 whole robot · divider board (companion to the system diagram, Fig. 1)

7-channel divider board build drawings

Perfboard 7×9 cm (24×32 holes @2.54 mm): 7 channels of 10k/10k dividers + 100 nF filtering, adapting the 5 V-domain output of the XGZP6847A (0.5–4.5 V) to two 3.3 V-powered ADS1115 modules (their header pins soldered directly onto the board). All analog routing stays on the board; off-board there are only 7 sensor lines in (S1–S7) + 1 Pi harness out (J1). No breadboards / Dupont plugs anywhere on the robot.

Assembly order and inspection checklist
  1. Bus wires first: 5 V rail along the top edge, GND rail along the bottom (tinned copper wire pulled straight and soldered along its length) + J1's 5 V/GND front-side traces.
  2. Resistors: R_A/R_B ×16 (1% metal film), spot-check each with a multimeter before soldering.
  3. Capacitors: 100 nF ×8 + 10 µF electrolytic at the entry (mind the polarity, + to 5 V).
  4. ADS1115 ×2: solder the header pins to the modules first, then solder the modules onto the board — no sockets, no Dupont; solder the ADDR→VDD jumper wire of ADS#2 at the same time.
  5. Back-side jumpers: divider node→AIN ×8, I2C/3.3V/GND daisy chain, all in insulated wire; never scrape and bridge at crossings.
  6. Connectors last: J1 and S1–S7 (JST-XH or soldered + heat-shrink); harnesses pass through cable-tie holes where they leave the board for strain relief; connector wires carry no pull.
  • Two multimeter checks before fitting (each channel): S-port OUT pad → divider node ≈10k; divider node → GND rail ≈10k. A clear deviation = cold joint / misplacement / short.
  • Powered check: i2cdetect -y 1 should show both 0x48 and 0x49; with 7 sensors connected and open to air, the 7 readings should be ≈ half the sensors' atmospheric output (≈0.5 V), i.e. ≈0.25 V and consistent across channels.
  • After checking channel numbers against the map one by one, label the board and both wire ends with a marker.
