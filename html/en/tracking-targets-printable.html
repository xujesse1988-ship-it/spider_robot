For NCC template matching and sub-pixel tracking · raises body_score · tolerates small in-plane rotation

🖨️ Print settings (very important):
1. Open this file in a browser and press Cmd + P (Mac) or Ctrl + P (Windows) to open the print dialog.
2. Set Scale to "100%" or "Actual Size"; never tick "Fit to page" or "Fit to paper".
3. After printing, first measure the 100 mm calibration ruler below with a ruler. If it is exactly 10.0 cm, the printed targets are 100% to scale.
4. Cut out a target of suitable size and stick it flat on the PCB with double-sided tape. Do not put clear tape over the front, to avoid reflections.
